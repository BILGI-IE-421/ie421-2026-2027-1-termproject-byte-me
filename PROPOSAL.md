## The problem

Popular music operates in a global market where songs compete for attention while digital platforms make music accessible across national borders. We want to investigate whether hit songs are becoming more short-lived over time and whether musical tastes across countries are becoming increasingly homogeneous. By combining historical Billboard Hot 100 charts with Spotify's country-level charts, we will examine changes in hit lifecycles and patterns of global music consumption. Our findings will help us critically explore the relationship between platform capitalism, consumer culture, and cultural homogenization.

## Our questions

1. How has the typical lifespan of a hit song on the Billboard Hot 100 changed historically?
2. How similar are music tastes across countries, and which countries diverge?
3. How does going global on a massive scale (multi-country presence) shape a song's success and longevity?
